# 🍺 Alkolender

A beer advent calendar tracker for friends who enjoy great beer, questionable rating decisions, and far too many statistics.

🌐 **Live site:** [Alkolender](https://helmermis.github.io/alkolender/)

## Features

- 🎄 Track beers by day and year
- ⭐ Rate beers and see group rankings
- 🏆 Best and worst beers of all time
- 😠 Hater Barometer and rating analytics
- 🤝 Taste compatibility between participants
- 🍻 Buyer rankings and performance tracking
- 🍺 Beer style statistics
- 📈 Alcohol percentage analysis
- 💰 Price-to-quality insights
- 🚩 Annual flag quiz competition
- 📅 Historical year-over-year comparisons
- 📊 Excel export for advanced analysis

## Purpose

Alkolender turns your annual beer advent calendar into a collection of statistics, rivalries, and memorable moments.

Whether you're hunting for the perfect Christmas beer or identifying the group's biggest hater, Alkolender has the data to prove it.
